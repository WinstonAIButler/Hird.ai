The autonomy dial

Set trust per task, not per agent.

Every kind of work has its own setting. Start cautious and turn it up as Hird earns it. The ledger shows you when it is ready: after 20 approved drafts in a row, Hird suggests the next level.

Questions

Before you hand over the keys.

What can Hird do without asking me?

Only what you set to Act on the autonomy dial. Every task type starts at Draft, and anything involving money, code merges or a first message to a new contact starts at Ask.

Can I undo something Hird did?

Yes. Reversible actions such as calendar changes, ad budget moves, drafts and pull requests have a one-click undo for 24 hours. Sent email cannot be unsent, which is why outbound mail starts at Ask.

What happens if I run out of included usage?

Hird pauses non-urgent work and tells you. You can raise your cap or wait for the next month. It never bills past the cap.

Does the CTO push code to production?

No. It opens pull requests with tests on a branch. Merging is set to Ask by default, and your existing CI and review rules still apply.

Where do I talk to Hird?

In Slack, iMessage, Telegram, Discord, by voice note, or in the web app. Approvals arrive as one-tap cards wherever you are.

How is this different from building my own agents?

You get the roles, permissions, ledger, undo and weekly reporting ready on day one, and the team keeps one shared memory of your business instead of separate chats.
