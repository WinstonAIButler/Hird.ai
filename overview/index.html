Executive overview · November 2026

An AI operating team that shows its work.

Hird.ai gives founders and small businesses nine AI specialists that work inside their real tools. Owners decide what each agent may do alone, and every action lands in a ledger they can read and undo. This page summarizes everything built so far: the company, brand, team, product, technology and what's left to launch.

01 · The company

Delegation founders can actually trust.

Small teams want to hand off email, code, sales and the books, but they can't afford an agent that acts without asking or leaves no record. Hird is built around control and proof first.

Problem

Founders are the bottleneck

Inbox, follow-ups, bugs, invoices and hiring all wait on one person. Generic AI assistants help in a chat window but don't own the work.

Solution

A specialist for each job

Nine named agents share one memory of the business and work in Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Stripe, QuickBooks, HubSpot and more.

Edge

Control and an audit trail

A per-task autonomy dial, a full action ledger with 24-hour undo, and a Friday report in hours and dollars.

02 · Brand

Named for the chieftain's sworn shield-band.

A hird was the household band that stood around a Norse chieftain. The mark is a round Viking shield with a riveted rim, carved with the Elder Futhark rune Hagalaz, the letter H. The brand is Norse in look and plain modern English in voice. The company was first drafted as Saga; the Friday report keeps that name.

04 · Product

Three ideas that make delegation safe.

The product is designed so an owner can start cautious and hand over more as trust builds, with a record of everything along the way.

Autonomy dial

Every task type gets its own level

Everything starts at Draft. Money, code merges and messages to new contacts start at Ask.

WatchRead and report
DraftPrepare for you
AskDo it after one tap
ActDo it, log it, undo for 24h
The Ledger

A record of every action

Each entry shows the tool used, before and after, and cost. Reversible actions such as drafts, calendar changes, ad budget moves and pull requests have one-click undo for 24 hours.

Weekly Saga

A one-page Friday report

Hours saved, money recovered, what shipped and decisions that need the owner, delivered every week.

Channels

Works where owners already are

Slack, iMessage, Telegram, Discord, voice notes and the web app.

Security

Narrow access by design

Each agent has its own OAuth scopes. Customer data is never used for training, and workspaces are erased within 30 days of deletion.

05 · Business model

Published pricing with usage shown in dollars.

06 · Technology

A lean stack that runs on Vercel.

A static marketing site plus two small serverless functions. Chat runs on Claude, and agents use Anthropic's Agent Skills inside a secure code container to produce real files.
